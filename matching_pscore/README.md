# Economics Research Projects

11 comprehensive economics research projects with academic rigor and proper citations.

## Projects

1. OLS, IV, and Panel Data - Econometric analysis
2. DID Engine - Difference-in-differences methods  
3. Propensity Score Matching - Matching algorithms
4. Regression Discontinuity - RD estimation
5. Solow Growth Model - Growth theory
6. IS-LM AD-AS - Macroeconomic models
7. Business Cycle Filters - HP, BK, CF filters
8. IO Competition - Cournot and Bertrand models
9. Gravity Model - International trade
10. Auction Simulation - Auction theory
11. General Equilibrium - Walrasian models
